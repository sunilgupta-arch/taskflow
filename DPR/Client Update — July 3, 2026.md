# Client Update — July 3, 2026

## What's New

### Weekly Roster Planning (Internal Team Side)

Previously, every team member had one fixed day off each week, and the only way to change it was on the day itself — if someone had to work their usual day off, they'd get a credit to take another day off later. This worked, but it meant managers had no way to plan coverage ahead of time — everything was handled reactively, day by day.

Now, managers can plan the team's days off a full week in advance:

- **Team members can request a specific day off** for the upcoming week, right from their attendance page.
- **Managers get a planning view** showing everyone's usual day off alongside any requests that have come in, and can assign each person's day off for the week with a couple of clicks.
- Once a week is published, everyone can see their confirmed day off in advance — no more surprises.
- If plans need to change later, managers can adjust the schedule at any time, and the affected team member is automatically notified.

**What stays the same:** if someone's planned day off still ends up being a working day for them (last-minute deadline, coverage gap, etc.), the same "did you work today?" prompt appears and they still earn a credit to take another day off later — nothing changes there, it just now works off whatever was actually planned for that week instead of their old fixed default.

This should reduce the amount of last-minute scrambling for coverage, since days off can now be planned around the team's actual workload instead of defaulting to a fixed schedule every week.
